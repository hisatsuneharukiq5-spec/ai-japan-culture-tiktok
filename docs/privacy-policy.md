**Last Updated: March 5, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

AI Japan Culture ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our TikTok application and related services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** If you create an account, we may collect your username, email address, and profile information
- **Communication Data:** Messages or inquiries you send to us
- **Video Content:** Any videos or content you interact with or create using our application

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** How you interact with the application (views, likes, shares, comments)
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, browser type
- **Log Data:** IP address, access times, pages visited, referring URL

### 2.3 Information from TikTok
With your consent, we may collect data from your TikTok account through their official APIs to provide services such as:
- Video analytics and performance metrics
- Audience engagement data
- Account statistics

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- **Providing Services:** To deliver, maintain, and improve our application
- **User Experience:** To personalize and enhance your experience
- **Analytics:** To understand how users interact with our content
- **Communication:** To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
- **Legal Compliance:** To comply with legal obligations
- **Security:** To detect and prevent fraud or security threats

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share information:

- **With TikTok:** As required by their API terms when providing our services
- **Service Providers:** With third-party services that assist us (hosting, analytics)
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers:** In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

- **Access:** Request access to your personal data
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten)
- **Opt-Out:** Opt-out of marketing communications
- **Portability:** Request a copy of your data in a portable format

Our application is not intended for individuals under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.
